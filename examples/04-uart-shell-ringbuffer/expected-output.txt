Example 04: UART shell (type help)
> help
Commands:
  help - list commands
  led - led on|off|toggle (PC13)
  clock - show the bus clocks
  mco - mco hse|pll|off: clock out on PA8
  uptime - milliseconds since start
  regs - dump RCC and FLASH registers
> clock
SYSCLK/HCLK 72000000 Hz, PCLK1 36000000 Hz, PCLK2 72000000 Hz
