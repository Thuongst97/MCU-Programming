Example 04: UART shell (type help)
> help
Commands:
  help - list commands
  led - led on|off|toggle (LD2, PA5)
  clock - show the bus clocks
  mco - mco hsi|sys|off: clock out on PA8 (D7)
  uptime - milliseconds since start
  regs - dump RCC and FLASH registers
> clock
SYSCLK/HCLK 170000000 Hz, PCLK1 170000000 Hz, PCLK2 170000000 Hz
